Modernized e-File (MeF) Application to Application (A2A) Certificate Changes

Effective July, 2018 the list of permitted X509 certificates is changing.  Going forward, externals may only use those certificates and intermediaries who are listed below. These are still part of the General Services Administration (GSA) bridge. 

The new list of certificates and how to acquire those is stated below.

Identrust IGC Medium Device Go to  Identrust

  • Select the "My Buying Community" pulldown, and click on "Government"
  • Select "Federal, State and Local Agencies" and choose "Buy Now" (if you prefer to use "Learn More", "Buy Now" is available on that screen as well and leads to the same destination).
  • In the list of agencies which takes up most of that screen, check the box next to "Department of Treasury - IRS MeF e-File" and then hit Next.
  • Please Note: Identrust has indicated that the user who applies for this certificate may need to have an IGC Medium Assurance Affiliate certificate as well, although such certificates should not normally be used by an application program logging into MeF

    • If you have to get the IGC Medium Assurance Affiliate type as well, you reach it by the following procedure: Go to Identrust

  • Select the "Certificates" pulldown, and click on "Federal Bridge Certified"
  • Choose "Buy Now"
  • Check the box whose descriptive text ends with "affiliation with my employer" and then hit Next.

ORC ECA, naming a server: Go to ORC ECA and select "Order Component/Server Certificates".

Please contact the MeF Mailbox for any questions or concerns you may have regarding this.
