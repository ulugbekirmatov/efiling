Not started · added Nov 9

Register Pyramos with Social Security as a third-party W-2 submitter

To upload W-2s for client companies, Pyramos needs its own Business Services Online (BSO) registration as a third-party submitter. Social Security’s new authorization process started Nov 7. An officer who can sign for Pyramos registers the company, answers yes to “payroll service provider”, and Social Security mails a registration code to Pyramos’s IRS address in about 15 days. Most clients sign nothing at Social Security. A client that registered itself under the new process, or opted in to the new Employer Signature PIN, must name Pyramos as its submitter and give us the PIN. Accounts on the old process must re-register for W-2 uploads. The SSN check (SSNVS) is not affected.

NextRegister this week so the code arrives before uploads open on Dec 8. Add a second Pyramos user. Ask each client whether it registered with Social Security or opted in to the Signature PIN.

Did the IRS accept the client Form 8655s?

Each client signs a Form 8655 so that Pyramos can file its 941 and 940 as Reporting Agent. The forms go to the IRS Ogden Reporting Agent team with the Reporting Agent’s List, by mail or by fax to 855-214-7523 (25 forms at most per fax). The IRS sends back a validated list within about 30 days. We have no record of a validated list, so we cannot yet say the IRS accepted any 8655.

NextFind the fax confirmations and any validated list from the IRS. If no list came back within 30 days of sending, call the IRS to confirm which client EINs are on file. Do not file a client’s first real return until that client is on the validated list.

Decision needed · added Nov 9

California payroll tax e-filing (EDD DE 9, DE 9C, DE 88)

California requires it. Since 2018, every employer and every payroll agent must e-file state payroll returns and pay deposits electronically with the California Employment Development Department (EDD). The penalties are $50 per return, $20 per wage item, and 15% of a late payment. Whether Pyramos does this work depends on the scope we choose: California state returns for clients, or only the federal 941 and 940. For 2026 the state returns stay with the current payroll channel, and none of this is built. California ACA reporting is a separate program and is already in production.

NextThe owner decides on the scope. If Pyramos takes on California, it registers with EDD as a payroll agent (forms DE 973B and DE 972) and keeps each client’s DE 48 authorization on file. Returns then go through one of two channels. EDD’s bulk filing channel needs enrollment on form DE 547 and a certification test. The e-Services file upload needs no certification. The DE 9C also covers California W-2 reporting, so no separate W-2 file goes to the state.
