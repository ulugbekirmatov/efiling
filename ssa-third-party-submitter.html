01

The problem

Pyramos is a Form 8655 reporting agent. It already sends ten clients' Forms 941 to the IRS under its own EFIN. W-2 Copy A does not go to the IRS. It goes to the Social Security Administration as one fixed-width EFW2 file, uploaded by a person through Business Services Online.

If SSA treated this like the IRS, every client would need its own enrollment: ten identity checks, ten activation codes in ten mailboxes, ten logins to babysit every January. The repo's wage file writer already assumes the opposite. It stacks ten RE blocks under one RA record. The question is whether SSA agrees.

03

The rule in plain words

What SSA says

A third-party submitter is a person who proves their own identity, names their own company, and attests. SSA sends the code to that company. Permission from the client is a private matter between the two of them. SSA never asks for a form, a letter, or a client login.

From 2026-11-07 a client may register an officer and restrict filing to named submitters, and may add a yearly Signature PIN. A client that does neither is filed exactly as before.

Every BSO user must register personally. YOU MAY NOT REGISTER ON BEHALF OF ANOTHER PERSON. Even if you are working on behalf of a client, register as yourself.Registration and Access to Services Handbook, p. 6
Check the Third-Party checkbox only if you are working on behalf of another business (EXAMPLE: Payroll company submitting wages for several different corporations. Use your own company's information, not the information for your client.)Registration and Access to Services Handbook, p. 17
Do I need to register for a BSO credential if I have a 3rd party submitting my W2's? No, you do not need to register for a BSO credential, although your third-party submitter will need to register for a credential.What's New For 2026, FAQ
The new BSO authorization process provides employers with the ability to assign and delegate who can submit wage reports and do business on their behalf. Delegated users may include third-party submitters if designated by the REO.BSO Authorization Information Session deck, 2026
If an employer opts in, then both they, and any third-party submitters submitting wage reports on the employer's behalf, will be required to include the Employer Signature PIN in the wage report for the Social Security Administration to process the wage report.Employer Signature PIN page

Who does what

05

What changed today

SSA's old process closed at 6 pm Eastern yesterday. Organizations on it must re-register for Wage File Upload, AccuWage Online, Submission Status, and Employer Report Status. SSNVS is not affected. SSA's Spring 2026 FAQ says the new process is not mandatory this tax year, and the pilot deck says the point of registering is to restrict who files for you. In the pilot, 70 percent of employers chose to restrict, and 25 percent of participants were payroll providers.

SSA's own roles, from the pilot deck. The REO is "an individual who is authorized to execute agreements on behalf of the entity that are legally binding and enforceable." The REO can appoint an Entity Business Administrator, who can appoint an Entity Service Administrator, who can appoint service users. Any of them with the right can name a third-party submitter.

06

What Pyramos does now

6 steps
  1. A Pyramos officer who can sign for the LLC creates a Login.gov or ID.me identity and registers Pyramos Software LLC on ssa.gov/bso as a payroll service provider.Start today. The pilot averaged 15 days for the mailed code. BSO opens 2026-12-08.
  2. When the code arrives, the officer enters it and adds a second Pyramos user with the wage reporting role.The old BSO User ID left with the employee. The new role model fixes that only if a second person is delegated.
  3. Ask each of the ten clients: has your company registered a Responsible Entity Officer with SSA, and did you opt in to the Employer Signature PIN?A client that did either must delegate Pyramos by company name and EIN and hand over this year's PIN.
  4. Add a nullable per-client Signature PIN to EmployerWage and write it at RE 319 to 328 with the Luhn check. Blanks when the client did not opt in.Ohio's state file reserves RE 319 to 512 as blank. Keep the federal and state writers separate.
  5. Call the Employer Reporting Service Center, 1-800-772-6270, and ask whether an unregistered client can still be filed by a registered third party under the new process.Same call: confirm a Form 8655 agent leaves the RE Agent Indicator blank and puts the client EIN at 8 to 16.
  6. Attend the SSA and IRS fall wage reporting meeting on 2026-11-10 at 1 pm Eastern.SSA says more guidance on the new process is coming. This is the first session after it went live.
07

Still open

Not answered by any SSA page read today
  1. "Not mandatory" versus "must re-register." The Spring 2026 FAQ says the new process is optional this year. Today's BSO page says old-process organizations must re-register for Wage File Upload. Both are SSA statements. Treat re-registration as required for Pyramos and optional for the clients until SSA says otherwise.
  2. An unregistered client under the new process. The pilot material frames registration as a way to restrict access, which implies an unrestricted EIN is still filable by any registered submitter. No page states that outright.
  3. The RE Agent Indicator for a Form 8655 agent. Publication 42-007 never uses the words "reporting agent." Position 7 codes exist for Form 2678 agents, common paymasters, and Section 3504 agents only. The writer leaves it blank. Still an inference.
  4. The handbook is stale. The registration handbook and the how-to-register deck still describe the Third-Party checkbox flow. SSA says it is updating its tutorials. The REO screens in the pilot deck are the newer picture.
08

Terms

Business Services Online, BSO
SSA's employer portal. Wage File Upload, AccuWage Online, and SSNVS live there. It has no test environment. Every upload is a live filing.
Third-party submitter
SSA's name for a company that files wage reports for other employers. Registered with its own EIN, not the clients'.
Responsible Entity Officer, REO
The person who registers an organization under the new process. Must be able to legally bind the entity. Receives the one mailed registration code and can delegate every other role.
Entity Business Administrator and Entity Service Administrator
Delegates under the REO. The first manages roles and services for people or third-party submitters. The second manages a subset of services for service users.
Employer Signature PIN
A yearly code in the form S-XXXXXX-Y, six digits plus a Luhn check digit, that an employer can opt in to from 2026. If opted in, every file for that employer must carry it in RE positions 319 to 328.
RE record
The employer record in an EFW2 file. One per client. The EIN at positions 8 to 16 is where SSA posts the wages that follow.
IAL2
NIST Identity Assurance Level 2. The identity-proofing bar Login.gov and ID.me meet for BSO. It is the only verification SSA describes for a third-party submitter.
EWRWS
SSA's server-to-server wage reporting web service. Closed to new consolidators. The reason the upload is a browser step, not an API call.
