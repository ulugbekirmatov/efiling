What landed in code

NewSendManifestXml.render is the live ATS path. The legacy SubmissionService emitter got the same two fields so the pattern cannot regress on that path either.

  <EFIN>102192</EFIN>
  <TaxYr>2026</TaxYr>

TaxYr sits after EFIN, matching efileAttachments.xsd. The XSD marks it optional. R0000-143 Reject and Stop requires a value. Year comes from taxPeriod.end().getYear().
